GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before we start.

Still wondering about something?
Susan is happy to talk it through.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. Tell us about your business in your own words. We’ll explain the options and guide you through the decisions.

How long will my website take?

Timing depends on the size of your site, the content you have ready, and feedback. We’ll agree on a realistic timeline in your quote.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, services, logo if you have one, and any photos or text you want to use. We’ll give you a clear checklist and discuss help if you need it.

Are the prices final?

These are illustrative starting prices, not binding offers. Your written quote confirms pages, revisions, integrations, taxes, and any recurring costs before you commit.

Can you help with my domain and hosting?

Yes, we can guide setup and connection. Domain ownership, registrar access, hosting fees, and responsibilities are confirmed before launch. Domain and hosting fees are not included unless your quote says so.

Will I be able to update my website?

We’ll discuss the best editing setup for your needs before building. You can also ask about our optional website care service.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. We can make your pages clear to visitors and search engines, but rankings depend on many factors. We don’t promise positions, traffic, or sales results.
